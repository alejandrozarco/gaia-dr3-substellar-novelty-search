## Caveats

1. **Log g discrepancy**: gspphot 2.90 vs gspspec 3.94. If gspspec right,
   M_1 might be lower (~1.5 M_sun) and the K_1 + astrometric tension
   reduces. Resolve via 1 high-S/N optical spectrum.
2. **e = 0.66 makes RV phase coverage harder** than the TYC 1363
   alternative. Need careful epoch placement near periastron.
3. **Astrometric mass overestimate** could be ~50-80% if F-subgiant
   chromatic bias is intermediate between the K3 III case (2×) and a
   pure dwarf (no bias). True M_2 likely 4-5 M_sun.
