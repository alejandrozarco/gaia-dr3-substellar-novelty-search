| BLS depth at P_NSS | 0.042 (~4%) | dominated by systematics, not real |

**Expected ellipsoidal modulation (Morris-Gomel formalism):**
- a_total = 0.299 AU = 64.3 R_⊙ (at M_1=1, M_2=4.7)
- R_1/a = 0.012
- q = M_2/M_1 ~ 4.7
- A_ell (peak-to-trough at i=90°) ≈ α₁ · q · (R_1/a)³ ≈ 1.4 × 4.7 × (0.012)³ ≈ **11 ppm**
- At i=78° (best estimate from astrometry, sin²i=0.96): A_ell ≈ 11 ppm
- At i=60°: A_ell ≈ 8 ppm

**Conclusion: predicted ellipsoidal amplitude (~10 ppm) is far below the 30-ppm phase-bin precision floor in the TESS data. TESS cannot test the BH hypothesis via ellipsoidal modulation.** The observed 500-600 ppm modulations at P and P/2 are consistent with detrending residuals at the noise floor (3-4σ each — barely significant).

**Transit non-detection:** at a = 0.30 AU and R_1 = 0.78 R_⊙, transit probability is R_1/a = 1.2%. Non-detection is fully geometric and uninformative.

**Reflection / heating:** for a BH companion, expected reflection signal is zero (dark companion). For a luminous K dwarf companion of R_2 ~ 0.7 R_⊙ at a = 0.3 AU, reflection ~ (R_2/2a)² · A · (T_2/T_1)^4 ~ (0.7/64)² · 0.3 · 0.6 ~ 25 ppm — also below TESS threshold.

**STRONG_CANDIDATE — non-Validated OrbitalAlternative with predicted K_1 sin i ≈ 100 km/s.**

### 9.4 Risk factors

1. **OrbitalAlternative class is a known source of pipeline artifacts.** Halbwachs+ 2023 documented systematic issues with `OrbitalAlternative*` solutions, including biased eccentricities and inflated photocentre amplitudes for sources with marginal `significance`. Our source has significance = 20.7 (high), goodness_of_fit = 1.06 (excellent), and 524 good observations — these argue against a pure-pipeline artifact. The max |corr| = 0.98 is the strongest argument for caution.
2. **Reddening uncertainty.** The source is at b = +4.6° in the Galactic disk with E(BP-RP) = 0.14 mag. If A_G is underestimated by ~0.5 mag, the implied M_1 could shift up by ~0.1 M_⊙, but this barely affects the M_2 posterior (already mostly dominated by a_phot and P, weakly dependent on M_1 in this regime).
3. **Contamination from the 16.6" neighbour** affects TESS but not Gaia astrometry (Gaia resolves them).
4. **Independent pre-publication on ResearchGate** means the "discovery" priority is already partially claimed, though the preprint has not been peer-reviewed. A confirming HARPS RV would put a peer-reviewed publication firmly within reach.
5. **Eccentricity uncertainty (0.28 ± 0.10) is large (35%)** — this propagates into a ~10% uncertainty in K_1 prediction. Not a major issue for the qualitative confirmation.

### 9.5 Bottom line

**The catalog has 1 surviving compact-object candidate.** This source is the lone genuine stellar-mass-BH candidate in v2+v2-alt. It is independently confirmed as RV-variable by Gaia and is in the right archival follow-up regime (HARPS-half-night detectability). **A single HARPS RV epoch with K_1 ≥ 30 km/s would convert it to a peer-reviewable discovery; a contradictory K_1 < 5 km/s would falsify it definitively.**
