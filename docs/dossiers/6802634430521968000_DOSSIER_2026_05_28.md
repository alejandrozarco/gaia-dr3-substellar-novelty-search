**Three red flags on the NSS orbital fit:**

1. **Period 584 d (1.6 yr) is in the alias-prone region.** Gaia DR3 NSS validation (Halbwachs+ 2023) warns that Orbital solutions with periods near integer multiples of the spacecraft scan law or near 1 yr need RV confirmation.
2. **e = 0.871 is in the extreme tail of the eccentricity distribution.** Sahlmann+ 2024 (J/A+A/693/A164) identifies high-e high-significance Orbital fits as a major false-positive class — a few high-amplitude epochs near "periastron" can dominate the χ² and absorb non-Keplerian astrometric residuals.
3. **17σ disagreement between NSS plx (1.23 mas) and single-source plx (2.12 mas).** A true Orbital solution should _improve_ the parallax determination, not change it by 1.7×. Bailer-Jones rgeo = 462 pc (plx ~ 2.16 mas) and MSC distance 553 pc (plx ~ 1.81 mas) bracket the single-source value. Only the gaia_source plx is consistent with BP-RP and M_G → single G dwarf at ~470 pc.
