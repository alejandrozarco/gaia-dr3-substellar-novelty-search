## 4. Why NOT in DR3 NSS

NSS tables queried (via Vizier I/357 family): no entry in nss_two_body_orbit, nss_acceleration_astro, nss_non_linear_spectro, nss_orbital. Reasons:

1. **Period likely > 3 yr** — the Gaia mission baseline is 34 months (DR3). With dV_tan ~50 km/s and a separation of order ~few AU, the implied period is order 4–10 yr.
2. **G = 10.58 is faint enough** that NSS detection thresholds bite: the Gaia DR3 NSS selection function is heavily reduced for G > 10.5, especially astrometric orbit. Hipparcos completeness at V<9 means the Hipparcos cross-match worked.
3. **High RUWE (3.05)** indicates the single-source astrometric solution is poor, which is consistent with an unresolved inner companion contaminating the centroid — the kind of scenario where the formal NSS pipeline often defers to "single source" rather than emit an orbit.

**M₂–P degeneracy**: M₂ scales nearly as P (for P > ~5 yr). At the BH3-matching P = 11.6 yr the inversion gives ~58 M☉; at P = 3 yr (Gaia-baseline lower bound) it gives ~17 M☉ — still nominally "BH". **But every single one of these assumes the PMa signal is from a single unresolved companion, which is FALSE for this system: a resolved 8.1″ companion at G=11.88 is in Gaia DR3 (and WDS J15245-4645) and produces a long-period (10⁴ yr at projected 8.1″ × 261 pc = 2120 AU) component that itself contributes a fraction of the chi².**

The 8.1″ companion's contribution to the photocentric perturbation is small (it's outside the Hipparcos 5–10 mas centroid box), so the inner-blend signal is the dominant chi² driver — but the very high RUWE = 3.05 is a "warning siren" that the single-source astrometric model breaks down. **The honest interpretation: residual PMa is plausibly explained by an M-dwarf inner companion of M ≈ 0.4 M☉ at P ≈ 3–6 yr (consistent with Kervella's 5-AU estimate), not a BH.**

## 7. Visual companion check (CRITICAL FOR THIS TARGET)

**WDS B/wds/wds**: 1 entry within 30″ — **WDS J15245-4645, sep = 8.1–8.2″, mag1=10.48, mag2=11.80, PA ≈ 10°**. This is the textbook HDS 2168 pair.

**Gaia DR3 within 10″**: TWO additional sources beyond the primary:
- Gaia DR3 5998932761094102656: G=11.88, sep=8.09″ — **this IS the WDS companion** (mag and separation match).
- Gaia DR3 5998932765405994880: G=20.91, sep=9.86″ — faint background source (not relevant).

**Gaia DR3 within 2″**: only the primary (no resolved inner companion in Gaia imagery).

**Interpretation**: The system is a "primary + wide partner (8.1″, ΔG ≈ 1.3 mag)" hierarchical. The Gaia DR3 imagery clearly resolves the 8.1″ pair; the Hipparcos PSF was ~0.5″ FWHM and would have measured the primary cleanly, but Hipparcos *position* astrometry has a systematic when the 8.1″ companion is bright enough — this is exactly what produces an HGCA chi² signal in a known wide-resolved pair. The lesson from HD 37419 (same Pile A hunt, demoted for the same reason) applies directly here.

Furthermore, the **RUWE = 3.05 for the primary alone** indicates an *unresolved inner* perturber — i.e. this is most likely a hierarchical triple: primary + inner stellar companion at a few AU (the source of the PMa amplitude) + wide 8.1″ companion (the source of the HDS catalog entry).

## 10. Verdict + follow-up plan

**Verdict: DEMOTED.** The system is HDS 2168 — a Hipparcos-resolved 8.1″ visual double — and the primary has RUWE = 3.05 indicating an additional unresolved inner perturber. The HGCA chi²=134 is fully consistent with this resolved+unresolved configuration; no BH is required.

Specifically:

- **8.1″ companion contribution**: long-period (~10⁴ yr at 2120 AU projection); contributes a small but non-zero chi² fraction via Hipparcos photocentric blend.
- **Inner unresolved component**: the dominant PMa driver. RUWE = 3.05 is well above the RUWE ≈ 1.4 "clean single" threshold. The Kervella 2022 H2G2 M₂(5 AU) = 423 M_J ≈ 0.4 M☉ estimate is exactly the M-dwarf-companion regime.
- **No X-ray, no radio**, so no LMXB / accreting BH evidence.
- **58 M☉ "BH" mass is an artefact** of the long-period inversion bin (P = 11.6 yr) combined with the assumption that the entire PMa amplitude comes from a single companion. At P = 3 yr (shortest credible) M₂ ≈ 17 M☉, still nominally "BH" — but again, this assumes single-companion which the WDS + RUWE both falsify.

**Follow-up plan (low priority)**:

1. **Speckle / lucky imaging** at any 4–8 m southern facility (e.g., SOAR + HRCam, Gemini + 'Alopeke) to constrain the inner ~50–200 mas companion suggested by RUWE = 3.05. Photometric speckle of the 8.1″ pair is also useful for cleaning the photocentric blend.
2. **Single-quadrature high-resolution RV** at CHIRON / FEROS to measure the radial velocity amplitude — but the primary is only V=10.4, so a 1m-class facility can reach σ_RV ≈ 100–300 m/s in 30 min, sufficient to detect the kind of K ≈ 5–15 km/s that a 0.4 M☉ inner M-dwarf at 5 AU would produce.
3. **Brand the target as a "demoted false-positive used for methodology calibration"** — it complements HD 37419 in the same hunt and quantifies how the WDS+RUWE post-filter would have reduced the headline list.

**Operational lesson**: For chi²>25 ∧ no-NSS BH candidates, the WDS cone (30″) + Gaia DR3 2-arcsec cone + RUWE > 2 check must be applied BEFORE the M₂ headline number is computed. Adding `simbad_main_id like "HDS%"` and `simbad_visual_binary = true` to the step-4 filter would automatically demote this case, freeing the "headline 58 M☉ BH" slot for the genuinely-clean HD 157033 (see its dossier).

**Why this target failed the 7-step novelty protocol's check but should still be demoted**: the 7-step protocol confirmed the system is NOT in any published BH/NS/triple/planet catalog — that's correct. But "novel" is not the same as "publication-grade BH candidate". The HDS catalog entry and the RUWE = 3.05 both predate the candidate, just not in the catalogs the novelty protocol queries.
