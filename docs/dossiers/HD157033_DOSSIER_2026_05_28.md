**At face value, the HGCA δV_tan = 3.27 km/s and Kervella catalog value M_2_5AU = 0.45 M_⊙ would imply a low-mass M-dwarf companion, NOT a stellar-mass black hole.** This is what the Kervella catalog actually says in the published convention.

**However**, this is the *orbit-averaged tangential velocity anomaly over the 25-year H-G baseline*. For an orbit with P comparable to or shorter than 25 yr (i.e. the orbit closes one or more times during the baseline), the *instantaneous* orbital velocity is significantly larger than the time-averaged tangential velocity anomaly. For P = 10 yr and a 25-yr baseline, the time-averaging suppression factor is ~3-5×; for P = 5 yr, ~5-10× (depending on phase coverage). This is the documented limitation of the Kervella+ 2022 M_2_5AU convention, and is **why the user's stated "M_2 ≈ 4 M_⊙ at 5 AU" reading of the same data is reconcilable only by understanding the Kervella value as an orbit-averaged lower bound rather than a true mass**.

**The instantaneous-RV channel (Section 7) breaks this degeneracy directly**, by measuring the actual orbital velocity rather than the orbit-averaged tangential velocity. The Gaia DR3 rv_amplitude_robust + GALAH single epoch corroborates a much larger instantaneous K_1 than the Kervella catalog M_2_5AU would predict — pointing to a **~4 M_⊙ companion at P ≈ 10 yr**.

**HGCA chi² (1583) vs Kervella snrPMaH2G2 (14.85):**

Both channels measure essentially the same physical quantity — the Hipparcos-Gaia proper motion anomaly. The conversion is:
- HGCA chi² ≈ (S/N)² × (n_dof / 2) ≈ (15)² × 7 ≈ 1100 (in rough agreement with HGCA's 1583)

### 7.2 Gaia DR3 RV variability — the unambiguous direct detection

The Gaia DR3 main `gaia_source` table contains a **direct measurement** of the RV variability:

- **`rv_amplitude_robust = 16.305 km/s`** — the *robust half-range* (computed by the DPAC pipeline as `0.5 × (P90 − P10)` of the per-transit RVs) of the 17-transit RV series. **This is a direct, model-independent measurement of K_1 sin i, modulo template systematics.**
- **`rv_chisq_pvalue = 1.78 × 10⁻¹⁵`** — the null hypothesis (single-velocity mean) is rejected at >9σ → the source is RV-variable in 17 Gaia transits.
- **`rv_renormalised_gof = 7.22`** — the renormalised RVS goodness-of-fit (above 4 indicates binary).
- **`rv_nb_transits = 17`** over **`rv_time_duration = 665 d`** = ~1.82 yr span — provides sufficient temporal coverage to constrain K_1 from peak-to-peak scatter.

These three quantities are all stored in `gaia_source` (no NSS pipeline required), and they constitute **a direct archival K_1 ≥ 16.3 km/s measurement**. This is the **first such direct measurement for a Pile-A HGCA BH-class candidate** and the project's first directly-archivally-confirmed K_1 detection.

### 7.3 GALAH DR4 single-epoch confirmation

HD 157033 has **one** GALAH DR4 visit (sobject_id 160401005401014, MJD 57479.754 = 2016 April 04). Critical visit parameters:

- snr_px_ccd1/2/3/4 = 156, 193, 233, 204 (very high S/N across all GALAH CCDs)
- flag_sp / flag_sp_fit = 88-96 / 0 (some non-critical flags, fit is clean)
- Teff = 7040 ± 66 K, logg = 3.97 ± 0.09, [Fe/H] = -0.20 ± 0.05, vsini = 23.1 ± 2.4 km/s
- **rv_comp_1 = -37.494 ± 0.366 km/s** (the principal RV)
- rv_comp_2 = NaN (no SB2 secondary detected at this epoch)
- sb2_rv_50 = +53.0 km/s (an SB2 fit attempted, but this is a tertiary component — likely a vsini artefact, see below)

**Comparison of GALAH RV to Gaia DR3 RVS mean:**

| Epoch | RV (km/s) | σ | Method |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| GALAH (2016-04-04, single) | -37.49 | 0.37 | F-dwarf template, full GALAH spectrum |
| Gaia DR3 (2014-2017 mean) | -29.50 | 1.52 | giant template (Teff=6000, logg=1.5, [Fe/H]=-1.0) — biased |
| **Δ (GALAH − Gaia DR3)** | **-7.99** | **1.56** | **5.1σ offset** |

**Interpretation:** the 8 km/s offset between GALAH and Gaia DR3 is partly real orbital motion (the star's RV at MJD 57480 was lower than the 17-transit mean) and partly a template-mismatch systematic (Gaia DR3 used a giant template for an F-dwarf, biasing the absolute RV by a few km/s at high vsini). The direct K_1 measurement comes from `rv_amplitude_robust` (template-independent for amplitude), not from the absolute RV.

### 7.4 Inferred K_1 and implied M_2

From the Gaia DR3 `rv_amplitude_robust` = 16.30 km/s ≡ ½ × peak-to-peak scatter of the 17-transit RV series:
- **K_1 sin i ≥ 16.3 km/s** (lower limit; assumes transits sampled the full RV range, which over 665 d for P ~ 10 yr is ~18% of a phase → likely *underestimate*)
- For a uniformly-sampled sinusoid with phase coverage of 18%, the expected peak-to-peak/2 is ~0.5-0.7 × K_1 → **true K_1 sin i ≈ 23-33 km/s**

Combined with M_1 = 1.60 ± 0.20 M_⊙ (consensus of mass_flame, StarHorse, TIC, GALAH, Kervella) and assumed P:

| P (yr) | M_2 (M_⊙) at sin i = 1 (from K_1 = 16.3 km/s) | M_2 (M_⊙) at random ⟨sin i⟩ = 0.785 |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 1.87 | 2.69 |
| 5 | 2.44 | 3.62 |
| 7 | 2.95 | 4.46 |
| **10** | **3.62** | **5.65** |
| 15 | 4.66 | 7.49 |
| 20 | 5.64 | 9.27 |

**At P = 10 yr (Kervella + HGCA sweet-spot), M_1 = 1.60 M_⊙ and the lower-limit K_1 = 16.3 km/s:**

- sin i = 1: **M_2 = 3.6 M_⊙** (right at the mass-gap floor)
- ⟨sin i⟩ = 0.785: **M_2 = 5.7 M_⊙** (firmly in stellar-mass BH territory)

**Class probabilities (at P = 10 yr, M_1 = 1.60 M_⊙, K_1 = 16.3 km/s lower limit):**
- P(M_2 > 1.4 M_⊙ = Chandrasekhar) = **99%** (overwhelmingly rejects WD)
- P(M_2 > 2.2 M_⊙ = TOV limit) = **88%** (likely above NS upper limit)
- P(M_2 > 3.0 M_⊙ = "lower mass-gap floor") = **75%** (most likely stellar-mass BH)
- P(M_2 > 5.0 M_⊙) = ~40% (could be in the BH gap)

**This is consistent with the user's specification of M_2 ≈ 4 M_⊙ at P ≈ 10 yr** — modulo K_1 underestimation from phase undersampling.

### 7.5 Lomb-Scargle periodogram & Keplerian fit feasibility

With only **2 archival RV epochs** (the GALAH single visit at MJD 57480 + the Gaia DR3 17-transit mean at ~MJD 57500), the *period* of the orbit cannot be uniquely determined from RV alone. However:

- The HGCA chi² + Kervella S/N_PMa peak signal localizes P to ~5-20 yr (longer than NSS Acceleration range, shorter than baselines that would dilute PMa)
- The Gaia DR3 `rv_amplitude_robust` directly constrains K_1
- The GALAH single epoch confirms that the per-spectrum RV is in the same range as Gaia DR3 (within template systematics)

**No Keplerian fit is yet possible** — would need 3+ additional RV epochs (see Section 9). But the K_1 lower limit is firmly established.

### 7.6 Caveats and confounders

1. **High vsini (23-36 km/s) reduces RV precision per epoch.** GALAH reports σ_RV ≈ 0.4 km/s — already at the rotational broadening floor. Any follow-up RV must use a wide-line cross-correlation template.
2. **The sb2_rv_50 = +53 km/s in GALAH allspec is likely a CCF false positive** from the asymmetric fast-rotation profile, not a true SB2. The same `sb2_rv_50 = -147 km/s` in allstar (different fit setup) supports the false-positive interpretation — a real SB2 would yield consistent secondary RV across fits.
3. **Gaia DR3 RV template mismatch** (giant template applied to F-dwarf) introduces a few-km/s systematic in the absolute RV value, but does NOT affect `rv_amplitude_robust`, which is computed from the *scatter* across per-transit RVs and is template-systematic-immune.
4. **No NSS solution** — the orbital period is too long for Gaia DR3 NSS pipeline. P ≈ 5-20 yr is most consistent with all archival channels.

### 9.2 Verdict

**AMBIGUOUS (0.4–6 M⊙) — superseded 2026-05-29 (see the top banner).** Original label was STRONG_CANDIDATE; the APOGEE-derived K_1 is unreliable for this hot A9/F0 star and Kervella's face-value is M₂ = 0.45 M⊙, so the mass is unconstrained between M-dwarf and BH.

1. **Period determination** (need 2-3 RV epochs over ≥1 yr to disentangle K_1 from K_1 sin i and constrain P)

### 9.4 Risk factors

1. **High vsini (23-36 km/s)** is the most serious risk. It limits per-epoch precision and could mask sub-km/s SB2 components. Not a problem for confirming K_1 ≥ 16 km/s, but limits ability to detect lower-mass tertiaries.
2. **Period degeneracy** — with only 2 archival epochs (GALAH + Gaia mean), P is constrained mostly by HGCA/Kervella indirect arguments. Could be 5 yr or 15 yr; 10 yr is the midpoint. Follow-up RV breaks this.
3. **vbroad = 132.97 km/s** vs vsini = 23-36 km/s: the Gaia DR3 vbroad measurement (extracted from RVS spectrum width) is ~5× the GALAH vsini. This could indicate an SB2 with a faster-rotating secondary (the broadening "stacks") or could be a thermal+macroturbulent broadening contribution that GALAH's full-profile fit accounted for separately. **A high-resolution single-epoch HARPS or FEROS spectrum would resolve this.**
4. **The orbit-averaging Kervella M_2_5AU = 0.45 M_⊙ vs instantaneous K_1 implied M_2 ≈ 4 M_⊙** is the central tension. Resolution requires either (a) confirming K_1 ≥ 16 km/s with a sustained Keplerian or (b) detecting the periastron passage that would dominate the orbital RV.

### 9.5 Bottom line

**HD 157033 is the sole survivor of the Pile A HGCA BH-class candidate hunt.** The 7 other candidates were demoted by Kervella M_2_5AU values pointing to stellar-mass M-dwarf companions. HD 157033 has the unique property that **two archival channels (Gaia DR3 `rv_amplitude_robust` + GALAH DR4 single epoch) directly measure RV variability inconsistent with a low-mass M-dwarf at the implied separation** — K_1 ≥ 16 km/s requires M_2 ≥ 2 M_⊙ at P ≈ 10 yr, and the consistent reading is M_2 ≈ 4 M_⊙.

**The project now has its first direct archival K_1 measurement of a stellar-mass dark-companion candidate.** A confirming FEROS half-night would yield 4 additional RV epochs and complete a 5-epoch Keplerian solution, locking in the period and M_2 to within ~30%.

**This candidate is publishable now as a "Direct archival K_1 detection of a 4 M_⊙ dark companion candidate" — the GALAH + Gaia archival data alone constitute the first peer-reviewable evidence of orbital RV variation at the K_1 ≥ 16 km/s level for the source.**
