- **Did:** Compiled the dossier — Gaia DR3 NSS Orbital (astrometric-only) solution, FLAME/APOGEE/MSC primary parameters, GALEX/AllWISE SED checks, and a 4-epoch archival-RV NSS-locked Keplerian fit (1 LAMOST LRS 2016 + 1 APOGEE DR17 2017 + 2 LAMOST MRS 2023 across 3 distinct phase bins). - **Found:** P=597.36 d, e=0.404, sig=104.5; a_phot=1.952 mas, sin i=0.958 (i≈73°); f_phot=0.288 M⊙. NSS-locked RV fit gives K_1=17.49±5.93 km/s, γ=-22.80, ω=353.5°, χ²/dof=0.002 (dof=1). LRS epoch at φ=0.042 (periastron) is 0.37σ from the 3-high-precision extrapolation → consistent, rules out INCONSISTENT/triple. Constant-RV null rejected (χ²=12.47/dof=3, ≈2.7σ). f(M)_RV=0.26 vs f_phot=0.29 agree <0.2σ. At M₁=1.40 (FLAME): M₂≈1.27 M⊙ (WD/NS boundary) on the tight astrometric channel; loose dynamical channel M₂=1.28 (+0.69/-0.52), P(NS)≈42%, P(BH)≈2%. **Verdict MARGINAL_2CHANNEL** (upgraded from WEAK): degeneracy broken + both channels agree, but ≈3σ K_1 rests on one ±10.54 km/s LRS epoch.

(Generic project corrections — the Thiele-Innes cos i sqrt bug and sin-i inflation — are NOT the operative ones here: f_phot is a direct photocentric mass function and the dossier's M₂ uses the astrometric channel, not sin-i inflation.)
- **Found (demotion risk):** the Gaia DR3 MSC fit prefers a luminous 2-component (5901+5714 K) solution (logposterior_msc=684.8); if physical (two G/K dwarfs) the photocentre wobble is flux-attenuated and M₂ is overestimated → a normal SB2.
