### 2026-09-29 — CH G band in a cool ultramassive DA
- **Did:** Blind spectral-anomaly search; DESI cross-check; literature (100 pc, DAQ, J-PLUS)
- **Found:** Two independent spectra show a CH G-band-shaped absorption ending at 4314 A in a 6700 K, 1.25 Msun hydrogen-atmosphere white dwarf previously typed plain DA. If CH, carbon is present in a cool hydrogen atmosphere: a possible cooled descendant of the DAQ merger remnants, none of which are known this cool. Next: model-atmosphere check of CH band strength vs C/H at 6700 K and logg 9.15; ZTF rotation search; higher-S/N spectrum (user-side).
