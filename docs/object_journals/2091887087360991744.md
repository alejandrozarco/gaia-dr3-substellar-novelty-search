## Entry log
*Append-only, chronological.*

### 2026-05-28 — HGCA PMa hunt surfaces it as the brightest novel BH candidate (G=7.16)
- **Did:** ranked the HGCA Brandt 2021 chi^2>25 AND no-NSS pool; this source has the 2nd-highest chi^2 (2269) and was the brightest novel candidate, headline M2_med=4.3 Msun at M1=1.5 (P-grid centred 11.6 yr).
- **Found:** PMa amplitude 12.37 mas/yr; chi^2=2269; with the correct M1=3.29 (FLAME) the M2 median shifts to ~5-6 Msun — but every inversion bin assumes the PMa is from a single unresolved companion.
- **Provenance:** dossier `HD_173689_DOSSIER_2026_05_28.md` §3/§5; HGCA PMa Hunt 2026-05-28.

### 2026-05-28 — DEMOTION: catalogued multiple + Kervella stellar M2 + RUWE (Kervella/WDS correction)
- **Did:** ran the WDS 30" cone + Gaia DR3 10" cone + RUWE + Kervella H2G2 check (the demote branch the 7-step novelty protocol omits).
- **Found:** WDS J18451+3500 already catalogues a hierarchical triple — **Ba,Bb at 1.0" + BC at 7.1"** — and Gaia DR3 resolves the BC companion (G=10.98 at 7.37"). The HGCA chi^2=2269 is gigantic precisely because the system is a known close visual multiple. **Kervella H2G2 snrPMaH2G2=25.79 (unambiguous) with M2(5 AU)=2056 M_J ~ 2 Msun -> an early-A/late-B inner stellar companion** (self-consistent at P~5 yr, M2~2-4 Msun); RUWE=2.38 confirms an unresolved sub-arcsec inner companion. No X-ray/radio (no LMXB/accreting BH). Soft mid-IR excess (K-W3=+0.60, K-W4=+0.86) is a marginal Be/debris caveat only. The 7-step protocol returned TRULY_NOVEL (correct — in no published BH/triple/planet catalog) but "novel" != "publication-grade BH"; the WDS hit is a ~30-yr-old multiplicity flag the protocol does not weight. Operational lesson: for HGCA chi^2>1000, WDS cone + Kervella snrH2G2>5 + RUWE check should auto-trigger a "known multiple" branch. Reframe as a stellar-orbital benchmark / negative control (with CD-46 10032A and HD 37419).
- **Provenance:** dossier top banner + §7/§10; CANDIDATES.md:183.
