## Entry log
*Append-only, chronological.*

### 2026-09-27 — Deep dive: 63.68-min white dwarf with a red, phase-dependent modulation
- **Did:** public release; SED fit; Gaia colour-phase test; Roche and irradiation estimates; literature comparison; ATLAS forced photometry requested
- **Found:** low-mass (~0.2 Msun, 18 kK) white dwarf; redder at maximum light; red SED excess; a detached companion at this period must be at or below the hydrogen-burning limit; if detached, shorter than the shortest published non-interacting WD+BD (68.2 min)
- **Provenance:** docs/reports/valley_eb1_2026_09_27/j1949/

### 2026-09-27 — ATLAS 2015-2026: independent confirmation, coherent over 11 years
- **Did:** Folded ATLAS c/o forced photometry on the adopted Gaia+TESS ephemeris
- **Found:** Same period in ATLAS as the highest peak in both bands; maximum within 0.01 cycles of the ephemeris in every 3-year block from 2015 to 2026; amplitude larger in o (19.7%) than c (13.0%), i.e. red-rising as in Gaia RP/BP. Three independent data sets (Gaia, TESS, ATLAS) now share one ephemeris over 2014-2026.
- **Provenance:** docs/reports/valley_eb1_2026_09_27/j1949/atlas_fold.png

### 2026-09-27 — Ephemeris coherence, P_dot limit and companion radius
- **Did:** Fitted a quadratic ephemeris to 20 phase measurements (2014-2026) and a two-temperature irradiation model to the ATLAS/Gaia/TESS amplitudes
- **Found:** The clock is coherent: |P_dot| < 2.3e-11 s/s (3 sigma), consistent with the gravitational-wave rate for 0.2+0.07 Msun (-8.5e-14). The amplitudes require a companion at or near its Roche-lobe radius (~0.10 Rsun): an irradiation-inflated brown dwarf close to contact, or a Roche-filling donor (which at 63.7 min, below the H-rich period minimum, would need a He-enriched or evolved donor). A comfortably detached compact brown dwarf underproduces the signal.
- **Provenance:** docs/reports/valley_eb1_2026_09_27/j1949/ephemeris_oc.png
