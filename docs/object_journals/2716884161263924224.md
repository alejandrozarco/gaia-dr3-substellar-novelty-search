### 2026-09-20 — Surfaced by the shallow/short-duty EB lane; characterised as a new EA
- **Did:** BLS search of ZTF DR light curves for shallow short-duty eclipses; harmonic period refinement; held-out validation; trapezoid eclipse fit in the HJD frame; 7-catalogue novelty gate with coverage proofs
- **Found:** Detached eclipsing binary, P=1.9846349 d, T0=HJD 2458258.63656, zr 16.318->17.004 (0.686 mag), zg 16.816->17.515 (0.699 mag), T14=4.3 h (9.1% duty), V-shaped partial eclipse, flat out-of-eclipse at 0.020 mag rms. 701 zr + 594 zg clean epochs over 2695 d; 49 distinct eclipse nights. Uncatalogued as a variable everywhere checked; present in ATLAS only as class 'dubious' with all eclipsing-binary probabilities exactly zero, despite ATLAS recovering the period to 2e-6 d. TRUE PERIOD AMBIGUITY: alternate minima equal to within 0.08 mag (3 sigma), so orbital period may be 2x.

### 2026-09-20 — Imagery + diagnostics pass: depth corrected upward, eclipse found to be GREY, period correction NOT robust
- **Did:** Multi-survey imagery (PS1/Legacy/SDSS/DSS/2MASS/WISE/unWISE), BLS periodogram, season-stacked O-C eclipse timing over 1359 cycles, phase-binned colour analysis, per-night eclipse figures. Independently recomputed the O-C rather than accepting a subagent's result.
- **Found:** (1) DEPTH CORRECTED: mid-eclipse depth is ~0.78 mag, not the 0.686 previously quoted - the earlier value was a trapezoid depth averaged across a V-shaped eclipse. Measured directly: faintest-epoch depth 0.778/0.778/0.772 in g/r/i. (2) THE ECLIPSE IS ACHROMATIC to within 0.006 mag across three bands; phase-binned Delta(g-r) = +0.0236 +/- 0.0129 (1.8 sigma). This implies near-twin components, which STRENGTHENS the case that the orbital period is the DOUBLED 3.969274 d with two near-identical eclipses. (3) PERIOD CORRECTION NOT ROBUST: season-stacked O-C gives +0.206 +/- 0.057 s/cycle (3.6 sigma) but chi2/dof = 2.68, reducing it to 2.2 sigma after error inflation; a second implementation gave +0.29 +/- 0.06, differing by ~1.5 sigma. Adopted P = 1.984637 +/- 0.000005 d, supported by ATLAS's independent fp-period = 1.984637. No period change (quadratic 0.2 sigma, |Pdot| < 0.23 s/yr). (4) BLEND HYPOTHESIS CLOSED on PS1 imaging. (5) No mid-IR excess. (6) Persistent and stellar over a 72-year imaging baseline.
