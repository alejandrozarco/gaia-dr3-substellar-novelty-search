### 2026-09-28 — ATLAS confirms the 28.89-min period in both bands
- **Did:** ATLAS forced photometry at the star's position folded and searched blind.
- **Found:** 49.8391 c/d is the top peak in ATLAS c and o (FAP 3e-12, 4e-12; 1.9% and 1.4%), matching ZTF g/r and three TESS sectors. Period established by three surveys, ~4,250 ATLAS + 1,100 ZTF + 42,700 TESS points; amplitude near-grey; interpretation: rotation of a magnetic, spotted 0.77-Msun DC white dwarf at 59 pc (period uncatalogued). Next: spectropolarimetry/spectroscopy to test the magnetic-rotator reading (user's contact list).
