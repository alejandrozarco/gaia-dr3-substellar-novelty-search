### 2026-05-28 — Original "CONFIRMED_CV_DISCOVERY" (claimed first period + deep eclipse)
- **Did:** ran the CV-period refresh pipeline — ZTF DR23 BLS (7.2-yr baseline) + TESS S98 2-min SPOC phase-fold + 7-step novelty cross-check.
- **Found:** claimed P=180.05-180.77 min = 3.013 hr (BLS power 43,842, the strongest in the 14-target priority pool), a "71% median quiescent" TESS eclipse (i~80-88 deg), and default-prior masses (M_WD=0.80, M_donor=0.25 Msun). Period+eclipse+SU UMa subtype all claimed NOVEL (absent from RKcat/Bruch2024/Hardy2017/Inight series/Pala/CuPS-ETV). Verdict: CONFIRMED_CV_DISCOVERY, targeted as an MNRAS Letter.

### 2026-05-28 — RETRACTION: period and eclipse falsified on the identical data (PHOTOMETRY-FP)
- **Did:** rigorous re-analysis — outburst-MASKED Lomb-Scargle + BLS on the same ZTF DR23 data, and a proper test of the actual S98 2-min SPOC product (per-cadence S/N + permutation FAP), plus S5/S32 FFI net-flux check.
- **Found:** (1) **period not supported** — masked-LS FAP=1.0 at 180.77 min in all 3 bands; only 1-sidereal-day aliases survive; BLS best period disagrees across bands. The "power 43,842" was an artifact of folding outburst-INCLUDING data. (2) **eclipse falsified** — S98 per-cadence S/N=0.66; the folded dip is 3.37sigma vs a random-period null MEAN of 4.07sigma (permutation FAP=0.87); the "71%" was a per-cycle minimum-of-noise artifact; S5/S32 FFI give negative net flux (TESS cannot detect this source at all). (3) **What survives:** a known DN with 6 ZTF + 1 CRTS outbursts and a real 7-day g=14.41 2023 superoutburst (SU UMa). No orbital period, eclipse, or inclination. CV identity is prior (Drake+2014, Coppejans+2016, Gaia best_class=CV 0.911). Residual publishable value: an RNAAS-level outburst note.
- **Provenance:** dossier top banner; CANDIDATES.md:104-113; `scripts/crts_j051419_ztf_recheck.py`, `scripts/crts_j051419_spoc_recheck.py`, `scripts/crts_j051419_s5s32_etv.py`.
