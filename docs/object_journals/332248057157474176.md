### 2026-06-03 — Can a 3rd/4th body explain the weak fit (F2=+8.39)? — DR4-gated
- **Did:** Considered modeling the poor single-Keplerian astrometric fit as a hierarchical triple (3rd body) or quadruple (4th body) to lower the residuals.
- **Found:** Not fittable from DR3: a multi-body astrometric fit needs the per-epoch transit measurements, which Gaia DR3 NSS never released (only the fitted single-Keplerian Thiele-Innes parameters + the F2 summary). We have the symptom (F2=+8.39, RUWE=8.79) but not the residual data points to fit a 2nd/3rd orbit to. Even with epochs: a stable outer orbit is years-long and DR3's ~34-month baseline (~3.6 inner cycles) barely samples one outer cycle -> under-constrained, high overfitting risk; a 4th body is unmotivated. **Physics twist:** a successful triple fit most likely lowers the inner-orbit a_phot -> inner M2 drops, plausibly below the NS floor, dissolving the M_tot>M_Ch claim. So 'assume a 3rd body' is a REFUTE pathway, not a rescue. No HGCA/Hipparcos acceleration handle (too faint). **Resolution:** DR4 epoch astrometry enables a single-vs-multi-body model comparison (pre-registered confirm/refute); pre-DR4 the only archival handle is the CPM check for a wide resolved tertiary (it cannot see a close inner body).
- **Provenance:** docs/dr4_preregistration_2026_06_01.md
