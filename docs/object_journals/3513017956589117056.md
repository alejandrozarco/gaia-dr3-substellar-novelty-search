## Entry log

### 2026-09-23 — Referee-supported candidate magnetic white-dwarf binary
- **Did:** Lane M flag; NEOWISE period, W2, field controls, photocentre and proper-motion tests; ZTF limits; SED; eRASS; literature incl. Pelisoli+2025; independent adversarial referee reproduced everything
- **Found:** IR-dominated orbital modulation (W1 0.96 mag in 2010 falling to ~0.3 by 2022-24 at constant phase; optical r <0.14 mag) co-moving with the Gaia star; cool WD + M5-M5.5 required by the SED; P = 0.07968186(4) d, W1 max BJD_TDB 2459999.9470(12). Best label: candidate low-field magnetic WD + M5 binary (low-state polar or pre-polar/LARP) with IR cyclotron modulation. Previously: Petrosky+2021 period (EB flag), Pelisoli+2025 'unclear'. Needs a spectrum

### 2026-09-23 — Second external review; empirical colour test
- **Did:** Astra adversarial review with web search; comparison with 18,323 nearby M dwarfs of the same red PS1 colours
- **Found:** Astra: IR variability convincing; label 'infrared periodic variable and candidate magnetic white-dwarf binary', orbital period unconfirmed, VSX type VAR. Colour test: the blue excess is not a metallicity effect (g-r 0.63 vs 1.26-1.28 medians, 1st percentile 1.00 for old-disc stars) while M_z is normal, so a cool WD-like component is required by the PS1 + SkyMapper photometry; irradiated-companion and other alternatives remain to be tested with a spectrum

### 2026-09-23 — Deep dive: other surveys phased, WISE per visit, P vs 2P, mean mid-IR excess; third external review
- **Did:** Folded PS1, SkyMapper, DECam, VHS and 2MASS epochs on the WISE ephemeris; per-visit WISE flux fits; timing; coherent odd/even test with simulations and field stars; photosphere-subtracted mean excess; unWISE per-band centroids; Spitzer and GALEX checks; Astra review of the results
- **Found:** No coherent optical/near-IR modulation; mean W1 flux flat while the modulated flux fell ~3x (partial recovery mid-2024); phase of maximum stable; odd/even difference not significant (P and 2P open); mean flux 2.0x (W1) and 3.6x (W2) the M-dwarf photosphere; W1/W2 centroids coincide. Review: cyclotron emission favoured; field, orbit and Pdot not established
