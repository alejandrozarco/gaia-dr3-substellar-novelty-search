## Entry log
*Append-only, chronological.*

### 2026-09-26 — Gap-fill of the Gaia-period lane: 87-min period confirmed in ZTF
- **Did:** Targeted ZTF test at the Gaia frequency; per-band amplitudes; SDSS/BOSS spectra; eclipse check; novelty gate
- **Found:** Coherent 87.33-min modulation, red-enhanced (r/g 2.6), in a 0.3 Msun DA without visible companion: candidate reflection off a substellar or late-M companion (like WD 1032+011 / SDSS J1205-0242). Needs RVs or IR. VSX revision candidate.
- **Provenance:** docs/reports/gaia_wd_periods_gapfill_2026_09_26/README.md

### 2026-09-27 — Archival infrared check
- **Did:** WISE (CatWISE, unWISE, Legacy Surveys Tractor) against a white-dwarf photosphere scaled to GALEX/SDSS/PS1; near-IR catalogue search
- **Found:** No infrared excess in W1; companion earlier than about M8-L0 excluded; a brown dwarf (L5 or later) is allowed and not testable with WISE. No near-IR survey data.
- **Provenance:** session 2026-09-27

### 2026-09-27 — Per-exposure radial velocities against the photometric phase
- **Did:** Balmer-line velocities in the 9 individual SDSS (2007) and BOSS (2012) exposures; sinusoid fit at the photometric period, with the phase free and with the phase fixed by the reflection-effect geometry
- **Found:** Velocity variation at the photometric period, in the phase expected for an irradiated companion (zero crossing at photometric phase 0.96 vs 0.00 expected), K = 14.5 km/s (4.5-24.5), M2 sin i about 14 MJ (4-27 MJ); significance about 2.9 sigma. Together with the WISE limit (no companion earlier than about M8-L0), a substellar companion is favoured. Needs time-resolved spectroscopy.
- **Provenance:** docs/reports/gaia_wd_periods_gapfill_2026_09_26/j1022_rv_2026_09_27/
