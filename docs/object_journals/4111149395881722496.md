### 2026-05-29 — Re-verification: STRONG → AMBIGUOUS (the operative CORRECTIONS here)
- **Did:** Audited the mass chain and the RV inputs.
- **CORRECTION 1 — sin-i inflation (the project-guardrail correction that applies to this object):** the BH-mass reading inflated M₂ by treating `rv_amplitude_robust` as 2K₁ and inventing a small sin i. This is the same class of error called out in the scientific guardrails ("No sin-i inflation for dark companions") and in the sibling 5858574 demotion (CANDIDATES.md:150,159, where rv_amplitude_robust/2 was misused to invent sin i=0.53). Here the honest statement is that rv_amplitude_robust is a robust half-range (≈2K₁), **not** a clean K₁, and is unreliable for a vsini≈23-36 km/s A9/F0 star.
- **CORRECTION 2 — unreliable APOGEE RVs:** the task-HH "K₁≈47 km/s, M₂=6-17" optimistic end rests on APOGEE RVs that are unusable for this hot star (high Chi2RV≈7.9, spurious [Fe/H]=-1.45).
- **Found:** companion is real (Gaia RV p=1.8e-15 + Kervella PMa + APOGEE scatter), but **mass unconstrained between an M-dwarf and a BH (honest range 0.4–6 M⊙)**; Kervella's face-value is M₂=0.45 M⊙; RUWE=0.85 means there is no astrometric orbit; could also be a hierarchical triple. Verdict → **AMBIGUOUS (0.4–6 M⊙), NOT a confirmed ~4-M⊙ BH.** Retraction banner added to the dossier head; dossier body §7-§9 "K₁≥16.3 → M₂≈4 / publishable now" framing superseded.
- **Provenance:** dossier banner:4; CANDIDATES.md:48,51,164-181; scientific-guardrails (CLAUDE.md "no sin-i inflation").
