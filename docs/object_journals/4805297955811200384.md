### 2026-09-29 — Brown-dwarf test: not yet decidable
- **Did:** Prior-spectroscopy check (ELM Survey South II), SED with DA models, SDSS-V H-alpha
- **Found:** The white dwarf is a normal-mass 31.8 kK DA (spectroscopic), not an ELM white dwarf. The companion's infrared flux gives M5-M6 at the parallax distance or late M to early L at the spectroscopic distance; its colours are distorted, as expected for a heated companion. Decisive test: time-resolved spectroscopy over one 105-min orbit (white-dwarf K ~40 km/s for a 0.05 Msun brown dwarf vs ~105 km/s for a 0.15 Msun M dwarf; H-alpha from the heated face moving by several hundred km/s).
- **Provenance:** docs/reports/tess_2min_rest_2026_09_28/
