### 2026-09-27 — New 11.06-h period in a hot (XP DO) white dwarf
- **Did:** Triage of unregistered hot GF21 WDs with Gaia GLS periods; ZTF, TESS SPOC and FFI checks; common-phase alias test; novelty gate (VSX, SIMBAD, VizieR all-table, ADS)
- **Found:** P = 11.0599 h in Gaia (2014-17), ZTF (2018-25) and TESS 120 s (2021, 2023, 2025); semi-amplitude ~4-5%, larger in blue (Gaia BP 5.2%, ZTF g 4.0% vs r 3.1%), as in the spotted hot WDs (e.g. WDJ0800+6334) rather than a reflection effect. No catalogue or paper gives a period. No spectrum; Gaia XP says DO. Decisive next step: an optical spectrum (DO/DOA? UHE lines at 4655/5280/6068 A?).
- **Provenance:** docs/reports/hot_periodic_wd_2026_09_27/j0948_tess_spoc.png
