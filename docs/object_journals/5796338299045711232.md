### 2026-05-28 — NSS-Orbital super-Jupiter/BD-borderline workup + dossier (initial "TRULY NOVEL" claim)
- **Did:** Gaia DR3 nss_two_body_orbit solution + full SED + 4-sector TESS LC + multi-archive RV cross-check + dossier.
- **Found:** P=488.053±3.944 d, e=0.148±0.048, a_phot=1.56±0.16 mas, sig=33.06, RUWE=2.51, AEN_sig=184.0σ; M₂≈11-12 M_J (face-on) at M₁=0.15-0.17 M⊙ — squarely on the planet/brown-dwarf D-burning border, scaling ∝1/sin i (→16-18 M_J at i=60°). d=25.55 pc, faint (G=14.8); needs a 3.6 m+ facility (HARPS/NIRPS). No archival RV exists. The dossier headline called it "TRULY NOVEL" — based on a cross-match table that **omitted** M&A 2023 and BJ&K 2026 (SIMBAD showed 0 individual bibcodes at the DR3 ID).
- **Provenance:** dossier; queries reproducible from `/tmp/mdwarf_gaia.py`, `/tmp/dossier_pm_corrected.py`, `/tmp/mdwarf_tess_analysis.py`, `/tmp/mdwarf_orbital_calc.py` (dossier:268).
