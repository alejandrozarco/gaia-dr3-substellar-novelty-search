## Entry log
*Append-only, chronological.*

### 2026-09-24 — Hot featureless ultramassive white dwarf in SDSS-V DR20
- **Did:** SDSS-V visit spectra; GF21 photometric parameters; hydrogen-in-B template fit; novelty gate
- **Found:** No Balmer or He lines at S/N 16-24 despite GF21 Teff 38,970 K, log g 9.22, 1.31 Msun. Hydrogen-in-B fit prefers ~250 MG but at the level of featureless negative controls (not a detection). Analogue: BPM 25260 (Bagnulo+2024: hot DC, magnetic >~100 MG, ~15 min polarisation period). SIMBAD WD* DA:, MWDD DA:, Cheng+2019 high-mass list; no spectroscopic classification found. Needs spectropolarimetry.
- **Provenance:** docs/reports/exotic_atm_sdssv_2026_09_24/magnetic_byproducts

### 2026-09-24 — Photometric period 103.38 min, located on the white dwarf
- **Did:** TESS S65 periodogram and pixel test; ATLAS forced photometry 2015-2026 (period refinement, harmonics, per-block amplitude); Gaia DR3 epoch photometry (VizieR I/355/epphot); ATLAS at the 3 neighbours; Gaia variability tables, VSX, Steen+2024, Jestin+2026, ADS aliases; VHS/CatWISE companion limits
- **Found:** P = 103.3757 min (f = 13.929771 +- 0.000007 c/d), coherent 2014-2026 in three independent data sets: TESS S65 (FAP 3e-7), ATLAS (c 5.4 %, o 5.7 %, nearly sinusoidal), Gaia G (6.9 %, same phase as ATLAS). Gaia photometry resolves the neighbours; ATLAS at the neighbours shows only small anti-phase residuals, so the source is the white dwarf. Previously catalogued only as a GLS frequency in the Gaia DR3 vari_spurious_signals table (produced for all variable sources); VSX type WD without period; not in Steen+2024 or Jestin+2026; no ADS full-text hit. A featureless 39 kK ultramassive white dwarf with a 103-min photometric period is most simply a rotating, strongly magnetic merger-remnant candidate. The companion alternative is disfavoured: amplitude nearly flat c to o, no emission lines, VHS/CatWISE exclude M and early/mid-L companions. Needs spectropolarimetry (field) and time-resolved spectroscopy.
- **Provenance:** docs/reports/exotic_atm_sdssv_2026_09_24/magnetic_byproducts
