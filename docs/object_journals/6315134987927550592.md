### 2026-09-22 — Re-check: coherent 2.2511 h period and a blue excess; candidate white-dwarf + M-dwarf binary
- **Did:** Re-examined the full ZTF set (696 clean epochs, 2018-2025) with Lomb-Scargle per band and per half; SkyMapper DR4 and PS1 DR2 colours; eRASS:3 flux; GALEX, 2MASS, AllWISE; Gaia quality; source-level search of Schwope+2026 and Rodriguez+2025.
- **Found:** Period 2.2511 h in g, r and both halves independently; the 2026-07-02 verdict used the 2.48 h daily alias (power 0.02). Colours g-r 0.25 (SkyMapper) and 0.46 (PS1) with r-i 1.27-1.53, where a single M dwarf has g-r ~1.5. M_G 12.12 at BP-RP 2.30. eRASS:3 Lx ~1.1e29 erg/s. Not listed by Schwope+2026 or Rodriguez+2025; Freund+2024 give p-coronal 0.86.
- **Status:** the 2026-07-02 'ordinary active M dwarf' reading does not account for the colours or the under-luminosity. Candidate white-dwarf + M-dwarf binary, possibly a low-accretion-rate polar; needs a spectrum.
- **Provenance:** docs/reports/erass1_j1526_2026_09_22/ztf_fold_2p2511h.png; ledger rows of this date.
