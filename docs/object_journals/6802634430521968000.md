## Entry log
*Append-only, chronological.*

### 2026-05-28 — Relaxed-v2 producer surfaces it, then internal Gaia cross-checks demote it
- **Did:** built the dossier on the producer's tentative `dormant_BH_candidate` (M2~3.0 Msun at M1=1.5); cross-checked the NSS solution against the Gaia DR3 internal products (MSC, parallax consistency, single-star APSIS convergence, RV diagnostics).
- **Found:** the M2~3.0 Msun headline holds ONLY under three simultaneous assumptions — dark companion + NSS parallax + sin i=1 — and every one is undercut: (1) **MSC** prefers a G2V+K3V luminous binary at d=553 pc (logposterior_msc=589.07, flags_msc=0); (2) the NSS parallax (1.226 mas) is in **17sigma tension** with the single-source value (2.116 mas, = MSC/BJ distance), so the inversion uses a non-physical a_phot; (3) e=0.871 is in the Sahlmann+2024 extreme-e false-positive class. With the gaia_source parallax, M2~1.0-1.3 Msun (K-dwarf/WD, not BH). All single-star pipelines (GSP-Phot/Spec/FLAME/ESP-HS) returned NaN; the RVS auto-template selected logg=1.0 (giant) for a photometric G dwarf — both SB2 fingerprints. Novel (no SIMBAD entry; absent from every published binary/BH catalog), but **NOT a credible BH**.
- **Provenance:** dossier `6802634430521968000_DOSSIER_2026_05_28.md` §3/§4/§9; raw `/tmp/bh_candidate_6802634_*.json`; `data/derived/main_hunt_derived_v2_relaxed.parquet`.

### 2026-05-28 — RV discriminator defined (could overturn)
- **Did:** computed predicted K1 for both interpretations and proposed an RV program.
- **Found:** BH case (M1=1.5, M2=3.0) predicts K1~57 km/s at i=90; luminous-binary case (M1=1.0, M2=0.7) predicts K1~21 km/s; at e=0.871 both give a >15 km/s peri swing easily detected at G=13.35. The discriminator is amplitude: ~20 km/s => luminous binary; ~40-60 km/s => BH; <5 km/s => spurious NSS (permanent demotion). Recommended 2-3 CHIRON/FEROS epochs around the predicted 2026-08-11 periastron — low priority. Demotion stands on the available Gaia DR3 evidence.
- **Provenance:** dossier §6:160-172, §9 RV proposal:224-251.
