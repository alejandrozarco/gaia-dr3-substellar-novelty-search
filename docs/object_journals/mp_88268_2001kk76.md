### 2026-07-08 — Measurement → refit → referee confirmation (precovery campaign wave 3 + refit)
Found as wave-2 bycatch-style archaeology: the MPC arc ends 2004; HST was deliberately pointed
at the object in 2006 (Noll KBO-binary survey — cold classicals have the highest binary fraction,
which is exactly why it was targeted) but the astrometry was never extracted or reported. Our 4
measured positions extend the arc +1.94 yr at HST precision and are its first space-based
astrometry. Joint refit shows the classic short-arc pathology working in our favour: the arc-only
covariance was optimistic AND biased (~0.95″ miss at 2006 ≈ 4σ_2006; 36–82″ = 3.2–3.4σ at
2026–2040); the HST anchor collapses future search boxes 13–77×. By 2040 the true position exits
the old solution's 3σ region entirely — filing rescues the object from becoming functionally lost.
Caveats carried: fo runs without a JPL DE (absolute a good to ~0.2 AU); astCat=Gaia3 choice
deferred to SARC; CLEAR photometry deliberately excluded from the ADES.
