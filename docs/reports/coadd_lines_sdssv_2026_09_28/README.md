# Multi-line screen of the SDSS-V DR20 white-dwarf coadds (2026-09-28)

Store: full-range inverse-variance coadds of all BOSS visits (Astra XCSAO shift undone for in-stack visits) for 50,886 SnowWhite objects, native grid CRVAL 3.5523, 1e-4 dex, 4648 pixels (extract_coadd.py; coadd_store in the persistent spectra store).
Screen (coadd_line_screen.py): windows of +-150 A around He II 4686/5412, He I 4471/5876/6678 and the UHE features 4500/5290/5673/6068, normalised by a straight line through the outer 40 A; photospheric template from the 40 nearest neighbours in BP-RP (same class group, S/N > 8), two colour-halves fitted linearly (DA group) with +-25 A around the line excluded from the fit; matched-filter z for Gaussian absorption and emission (sigma 2/4/8 A; velocities +-400 km/s, +-1500 for UHE); errors include 0.15 (DA) / 0.05 (other) x neighbour scatter. 50,760 objects screened.
Controls: 52 MWDD DAO, 7 DAB, 112 DBA, 9 known UHE stars in the sample (coadd_controls.csv). DA null tail of z(He II 4686): 1st percentile -12.7, 0.1th -34.6; DAO controls median -12.0 (recovered 8/15 at z < -5 with He II 5412 < -3); known DAB 6/7 recovered; UHE controls not recovered (0/1 on the locus) - the UHE features are broader and shallower than the templates assume.
New-candidate filters (rank_lines.py and the tightened cut): SnowWhite class DA/DA: only, no helium type in the MWDD, white-dwarf locus, EW >= 1 A (DAB: He I 5876 and 4471; DAO: He II 4686 >= 1 A with 5412), UHE: BP-RP < -0.2, He II present, >= 2 UHE windows at z < -6. Survivors: DAO 18, DAB 72, UHE 12 (candidates_*.csv); visual vetting of the top entries.
Outcomes: new DAB candidates journaled: 866919567943315712 (He I EW 4.5-6.9 A, S/N 35), 1982816805194909952, 5673721037184764416 (hot, weak He II), 6655745926228714752. False-positive modes: known DAH stars (Zeeman-distorted profiles), single corrupted visits (saw-tooth flux jumps), single-visit flux-calibration ripples mimicking UHE troughs, DQ/hotDQ carbon bands in the He II window, cool composites in the He I windows.
