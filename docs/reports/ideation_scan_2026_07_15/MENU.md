## Lessons

(1) The remaining discovery surface for a solo is not discovery at all — it is PROPAGATION labor: professional teams measure, publish catalogs/detections, and move on, and the gap between publication and the codified registries (MPC, VSX, TNS) is the durable niche. Both of the project's best wins (SSOLS unsubmitted HST astrometry, GFCAT unregistered variables) and four of five GO clusters share this exact 'measured but never filed' shape — it is now the search template for future scans. (2) Demand-side target selection beats supply-side everywhere it was tested: pick objects a named consumer needs (occultation campaigns, HST scheduling, CODE catalogue), not objects an archive happens to contain — the closed precovery campaign's fatal flaw generalizes. (3) Consumer verification needs an INDEPENDENCE test, not just a named-consumer test: the symbiotic sub-lane's responsive consumer (Merc/NODSV) turned out to be the competitor running the same program with an embedded amateur. (4) Precedent analogies must survive depth arithmetic: Kai Ly's playbook failed to transfer over a 3-magnitude gap (single-exposure mag-23 vs stack-only mag-26); check the precedent's limiting magnitude against the target's before believing any 'amateur X did it' story. (5) 'Neglected tail' claims require an incumbent-CADENCE check, not just a scoop check: the Ashton team re-mined its own archive 4 months ago, and Deen precovers routine comets within weeks — a lane can be occupied by rhythm rather than by a paper. (6) Supply numbers from scouts do not survive contact: the referee's spot-checks found zero qualifying occultation events against a claimed 2-5/month, an order-of-magnitude-inflated ITF estimate, and two garbled citations — every headline rate now needs a same-week snapshot census as gate zero, and citation re-grounding is mandatory referee work. (7) Meta-products (labels, null measurements, benchmarks) carry clocks tied to instrument-maturity windows: the Rubin year-1 immaturity that the live lane exploits is simultaneously a publishable measurement that institutional teams (SkAI hackathon) are organizing to own — bank such byproducts as DOIs BEFORE the window closes, at near-zero marginal cost. (8) The AAVSO account has become the single highest-leverage pending user action in the portfolio: three GO_CANDIDATE lanes plus the approved VSX lane now queue behind it.
