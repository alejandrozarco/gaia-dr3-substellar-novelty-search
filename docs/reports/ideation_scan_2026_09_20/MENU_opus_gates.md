# Ideation Scan v6 — Final Ranked Menu

*Compiled 2026-09-20. Objective: NOVELTY discovery from archives, community-confirm mode, no telescope, VSX/TNS/MPC credit, 6-core box, Gaia DR4 on 2026-12-02. Ranked by `novelty_score / labor_score`, not by yield.*

---

## 1. Honest framing

Ten curated lanes went into adversarial gating and **none came out GO**: four GO_CANDIDATE, four MARGINAL, two NO_GO. Nine of the ten are the same species — *absence as signal*: a source is selected because an archive that should have measured it did not (ZTF non-detections, censored eclipses, missing Gaia transits, a reference co-add built during an outburst, a source absent from a later epoch). That convergence is not a coincidence and it is not a fad; it is what is left after the detection-based surface was cleared. But it comes with a structural tax the gates charged repeatedly: an absence is also produced by every pipeline pathology, so five of the ten lanes died or were downgraded not on prior art but on an **unmeasured false-positive floor** (Lane 1's pilot bounds its residual rate at <1e-3 against a parent of 2e7 — permitting ~20,000 false survivors against ~200-400 claimed real ones). The second thing the gates found is speed: two lanes were shot by papers **less than three weeks old** (van Roestel+2026, arXiv:2609.08923, posted ~2026-09-08), and three more by incumbents walking up one magnitude slice per year on the very population claimed as open. The remaining surface is therefore real but *thin and time-boxed* — it is the specific corner an incumbent's parent catalogue excludes by construction, exploitable for months, not years, and only if the pipeline can prove it recovers published positives on its own configuration before it is pointed at the sky.

## 2. The ranked menu (GO_CANDIDATE)

### #1 — Reference-poisoned outbursters (ratio 4 / 3 = 1.33)
*ZTF DR24 light curves joined to `ztf_current_meta_ref` reference windows*

**Selection, with numbers.** The gate pulled the table live rather than accepting the lane's premise: `ztf.ztf_current_meta_ref` = **196,357 rows**, span distribution p5 = 30.0 d, p25 = 93.9 d, **median 282.1 d**, p75 = 606.4 d, p90 = 1392.2 d; only **9,683 rows (4.93%) have windows ≤30 d**, and those short-window references have **median nframes 15**. Because references are outlier-rejected co-adds of ≥15 frames, a 10-20 d superoutburst contributes 1-3 frames to a median-282-day window and is clipped away — so **Tier 1 (dwarf novae, AM CVn, EXor) exists only inside the 4.93% short-window subset**: 3,199 zr + 3,456 zg quadrants, zr sky ≈ 3,199 × 0.73 deg² ≈ **2,340 deg², ~7.8% of the public footprint**. Working sample there: ~1,200-1,600 outbursting CVs known+unknown; requiring an outburst covering ≥half a 30-day/15-frame window at a ~5-10% duty cycle with phase alignment gives **order 20-60 poisoned systems, ~5-25 genuinely novel** — not the 100-300 claimed. Cut per object: ≥3 catflags-clean detections inside its own quadrant's window and ≥20 outside; in-window median ≥1.0 mag brighter; ≥80% of in-window epochs ≥0.7 mag brighter; reject Lomb-Scargle 60-1,000 d amplitude >0.8 mag; ALeRCE zero positive alerts 2018-2025.

**Data endpoints.** IRSA TAP sync `https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/TAP/sync`, table `ztf.ztf_current_meta_ref` — anonymous, 9 columns, full table in **48 s** (already on disk at `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-legbatterij-claude-projects/5bae4473-7497-4a6b-8bf6-3ecc9abd4295/scratchpad/meta_ref.csv`). ZTF DR24 HATS/parquet on public S3 — **pull only the HATS partitions matching the 3,199 zr + 3,456 zg short-window quadrants (~5% of the archive, ~1 night on enzo); do NOT scan all of DR24 (~3-4 TB)**. ALeRCE public API, batched at the ~5,000-object stage only, with explicit empty-200 checks. ZTF forced photometry (for `refmag`) is email-gated and returns browser downloads — the real friction point, finalists only.

**1-2 day pilot.** Take the 3,199 zr quadrants with windows ≤30 d; cross-match VSX dwarf novae and AM CVn systems falling in them; for each, check whether a known outburst epoch lands inside *its own* quadrant's reference window; pull only those HATS partitions and run the in-vs-out-window criterion unchanged.

**GATE ZERO.** Measure two numbers: **(a)** what fraction of known DNe with a superoutburst inside their own ≤30 d reference window are recovered by the ≥1.0 mag in-vs-out-window median cut; **(b)** for those recovered, does ALeRCE show zero positive alerts over 2018-2025. *If (a) is near zero, a 15-frame outlier-rejected co-add is simply not poisoned by a 10-20 d superoutburst → Tier 1 dies outright and Tier 2 alone (high-N, low-novelty) is not worth three weeks. If (a) is healthy but (b) fails, the causal premise is wrong and the lane dies regardless of yield.*

**Self-recovery template.** VSX dwarf novae / AM CVn with a documented outburst epoch inside their own quadrant's ≤30 d window. Second-tier control **only after the first passes**: the reference-contaminated SNe Ia of Dhawan+2022 (ZTF SN Ia DR1) — it tests a different tier and cannot rescue Tier 1.

**Credit channel.** VSX new-variable submissions (UG / UGSU / UGZ / ZAND / NL) with DR light curves showing the outbursts; AstroNote for recurrent blind systems (standstills, nova-likes); TNS archival reports for the 2018-window transient sub-claim — *that half is blocked on task #152 and, per the gate, cannot be produced by the lane's own selection criteria anyway (a faded 2018 SN has essentially zero out-window detections, failing the ≥20-epoch rule).*

**Biggest risk.** *N and novelty are anti-correlated and the lane does not admit it.* The 4.93% short-window tier has the novelty and yields 5-25 objects; the 55.77% with year-plus windows yield plenty of flags, but anything bright enough to poison a multi-year co-add is a persistent bright variable already in VSX/SIMBAD. Expected outcome is not a clean null — it is a few thousand flags vetting down to a couple of dozen new objects, against SNAD's measured ~1.5-uncatalogued-per-field threshold. Secondary: DR24 parquet lacks `refmag`, so the poisoning itself is never confirmed and the advertised population-level byproduct ("how many ZTF references are poisoned") **cannot be delivered by this pipeline as specified**.

### #2 — Faint southern dwarf-nova census, **Stage 1 only** (ratio 4 / 3 = 1.33)
*DES-SN5YR DiffImg release. Ties #1 on ratio; ranked second because its honest yield is smaller, but its gate-zero is the cheapest decisive test in the entire set — run it the same week.*

**Selection, with numbers.** The public DES-SN5YR release: **31,636 DiffImg candidate light curves**, griz, 5 seasons, ~7-day cadence, 27 deg² over 10 fields, 2013-2018. Cross-match to static point sources at quiescent r 21-24.5 in the DES Y3 deep coadds (r~26-27 in C3/X3/E2). **The gate recomputed the CV column density independently and the lane's headline is 2-5× too high**: Pala+2020 n0 = 4.8e-6 pc⁻³, scale height 280 pc, at the DES-SN fields' |b| = 45-65 gives N = 0.086 (b=65), 0.099 (b=60), 0.121 (b=54), 0.182 (b=45) CV/deg² — **27 deg² × ~0.12 = 3.2 CVs in the entire footprint**, full column, all magnitudes. With ~70% at r_q > 21 and some already in VSX/CRTS, the honest expectation is **0-3 new faint CVs, most likely 1**. What redeems it: DiffImg photometry is **forced at the mean candidate position across all epochs**, shallow fields reach r ~23.5 and deep r ~24.5 single-epoch, ~20 epochs/season/filter — so a 2-5 mag outburst lasting 1-4 weeks is caught *and its duration measured*, which separates DN outbursts from flares. **Stage 3 (the NSC DR2 meas extension) must be refused as written**: DES wide 90 s exposures are 10σ r ~23.0-23.3 and NSC DR2 median `depth10sig` in r is ~22.5, so a quiescent r = 23.5-24.5 source produces **zero** meas rows and cannot be an `nsc_dr2.object` with ndet ≥ 6 — the entire claimed period-bouncer yield rests on a channel with no rows.

**Data endpoints.** `github.com/des-science/DES-SN5YR` + **Zenodo 12720778** — a downloadable file set, no account, no rate limit, fits on the 6-core box, touches no per-position API. HELM DR1 (Zhang+2024, ApJS 274, 42; 2019A-2022A, 3-6 d cadence, COSMOS / XMM-LSS / CDF-S / ELAIS-S1 / S-CVZ / Stripe 82) served from an open HTTP directory — an independent 3-year recurrence window *after* the DES seasons end. DES DR2 coadds + Y3 deep-field catalogue and LS DR10 via NOIRLab, both public.

**1 day pilot.** Download the release. Take every VSX / CRTS / ASAS-SN known dwarf nova and known CV whose position falls inside the 27 deg².

**GATE ZERO.** **(a)** Do the known DNe appear in the 31,636 released candidates at all, with a recognisable outburst light curve? *If known DNe inside the footprint are largely ABSENT, the DES real-time candidate stream or the release packaging silently removed Galactic outbursters → the lane has no supply, dead at zero cost, and no NSC pull is ever attempted.* **(b)** Independently, count candidates coincident within 1″ of a static point source in the DES deep coadds that show one or more isolated 1-4 week brightenings rather than an SN-like single rise-and-decay. *If that count is ~0-3 and all are already in VSX, the recomputed 3.2-CV density is confirmed and Stage 3's 100× labor for shallower depth is not worth starting.*

**Self-recovery template.** VSX/CRTS/ASAS-SN known dwarf novae inside the ten DES-SN fields — recovery required *before* any search, per lever #7.

**Credit channel.** VSX (UG / UGSU / UGWZ:) with the DECam per-exposure outburst light curve and quiescent photometry — VSX accepts survey-only discoveries. TNS archival AT reports for hostless outbursts (TNS accepts past discovery dates), blocked on task #152.

**Biggest risk.** M-dwarf flares eat any Stage-3 candidate list: DES takes griz of a hex within ~10 minutes, so a 30-minute flare produces exactly the selected signature (≥2 same-night measurements ≥2 mag above median, PSF-shaped, same centroid, flags=0), and NSC meas gives no intra-night duration leverage beyond that ~10-minute span. **Stage 1 does not have this problem** — which is precisely why the value is concentrated in the cheapest stage. Secondary: the hostless branch's "no counterpart to r ~24.5-26.5 in a deeper dataset" is the deep-sharp-versus-shallow-coarse absence claim that killed the turn-on lane, re-imported with the roles reversed.

> "Sanchez et al. 2024, arXiv:2406.05046 (DES-SN5YR release: 31,636 DiffImg LCs, 27 deg^2, 8 shallow + 2 deep fields; multi-season AGN/long-lived cut applied downstream, not to the release; DiffImg photometry is forced at the mean candidate position)"

### #3 — Gaia DR4 day-1 single-dip search (ratio 4 / 4.5 = 0.89)
*Re-scoped: make the GOST censoring channel PRIMARY.*

**Selection, with numbers.** Verified against the ESA DR4 content page: release **2 Dec 2026**, 2.8G sources, 66 months (25 Jul 2014 – 20 Jan 2020), `gaiadr4.vari_summary` at **403,061,676 rows**, and **no single-dip / one-off-fading table among the 20 DR4 variability products** — CU7's Lomb-Scargle + multi-harmonic EB pipeline is structurally incapable of emitting an unrepeated event. Stage 1: `num_selected_g_fov ≥ 30`, median−min ≥ 0.4 mag, max−median ≤ 0.10 mag, skewness ≥ +1.5, depth ≥ 8× robust scatter → **1e5-3e5 survivors**. Detection efficiency with N≈65 transits over 2010 d: **T=1 d → 3.2%, 10 d → 28%, 30 d → 62%, 100 d → 96%, 1 yr → ~100%** — this is a *long-event* lane, i.e. it peaks exactly where Gaia Science Alerts is strongest. Yield recomputed from JoHantgen+2025 (16 objects / 5.1e6 stars / 10 yr = 3.1e-7 per star-year): 2.0e8 usable × 3.1e-7 × 5.5 yr = 341 events × ~0.5 duration-averaged efficiency ≈ **170 all-sky**, minus GSA's ~46% external completeness, minus the OGLE-IV/VVV footprints → **~50-90 residual, ~30-60 in the southern faint slice** (claimed 100-200). The "unique southern window" is **~1 magnitude (G~18-19), not 5**: ASAS-SN Chile ran V<17 from 2013 and g<18.5 from 2017. Purity: Gaia DR3 docs report ~30 per million sources with anomalous flux behaviour, spurious classes named literally *"big dippers"* — **~12,000 spurious against ~170 real, 1:70 before vetting**. **Re-scope: the GOST missing-transit channel becomes the primary product** (a >2.7 mag dip on G~18 falls below the G~20.7 onboard limit and appears as *absent* transits) — it is orthogonal to GSA, which needs detections, and it is the only part of the lane nobody else will run on 2 December.

**Data endpoints.** `gea.esac.esa.int` TAP (anonymous — **cap unverified**); fallbacks Gaia@AIP `gaia.aip.de`, VizieR/CDS TAP, ESAC bulk DataLink over plain HTTP; GOST for predicted visit times; DataLink `epoch_photometry` in 5,000-ID chunks (2e5 IDs = 40 calls).

**1 day pilot (run THIS WEEK, on DR3).** Execute the exact Stage-1 `vari_summary` query against `gaiadr3`, anonymously, from the 6-core box.

**GATE ZERO — three kills in one afternoon.** **(1) ACCESS:** does anonymous TAP return >2,000 rows, and can DataLink be pulled without a login? *The user cannot register accounts; a lane named "TAP-only" never checked whether anonymous TAP can do what it asks, and Stage 3 needs table-upload crossmatch. Settle this in 20 minutes rather than on 2 Dec in a congested archive.* Add an explicit integrity assert (row count > 0 **AND** a known-present control ID appears) so a throttled empty-200 cannot masquerade as a null — lever #4. **(2) TEMPLATE RECOVERY.** **(3) THE PRIOR-ART KILL:** cone-match clean DR3 survivors against the Gaia Alerts index (`gsaweb.ast.cam.ac.uk`, fully public) plus SIMBAD and VSX — *if >90% of vetted survivors are already named Gaia alerts, GSA has harvested this channel to Gaia's own photometric floor and the DR4 residual is noise; lane closed for one day.* Bonus: the DR3 run is itself a live discovery run on data public today. Second, cheaper-still test if all three pass: 200 DR3 sources with GOST-predicted visits, predicted-vs-actual transit counts, to see whether the censoring channel is separable from ordinary scanning-law gaps.

**Self-recovery template.** **TYC 2505-672-1**, EE Cep-class objects, and those of the **16 ASAS-SN big dippers** (arXiv:2507.19594) that have DR3 epoch photometry. *If the query recovers 0 controls while returning 1e4 survivors, the cuts are wrong and DR4 multiplies the mess by 40×.*

**Credit channel.** VSX with DR4 epoch photometry as evidence (EA with period unknown; DIP for dust/disk dippers), named credit per object; AstroNote for any class-level result. Filing in the first 2-4 weeks is the whole game — VSX form logic and drafts prepared before 2 Dec.

**Biggest risk.** *The lane's efficiency curve and Gaia Photometric Science Alerts' peak on the same events* — GSA has run a real-time all-sky **brightening AND fading** detector on these exact photons since 2014, all-sky including plane and bulge, publishing each catch with a Gaia14xxx–Gaia26xxx name; **the lane's 3,000-word rationale never mentions GSA once**. Most likely outcome is not a null but a few hundred hours of vetting producing a list whose every clean member already has a Gaia designation. Compounding: the real filter is environment-based (Hodgkin+2021 report environment-aware filtering cuts candidates by a further factor ~50) and the TAP-only design gives that up; the "require ≥2 FOV transits" gate is **not** a rule-10 analogue — those transits are 106.5 minutes apart and are not independent epochs.

### #4 — Post-Gaia northern microlensing in ZTF DR24 (ratio 3.5 / 4 = 0.875)
*Scope cut: bright fittable core only.*

**Selection, with numbers.** A precisely bounded empty catalogue window: **2023-03-09** (end of the Zhai+2024 DR17 search) to **2025-10-22** (DR24 public cutoff, MJD 60970) = **2.62 yr** with no DR-based catalogue. Post-Gaia sub-window (2025-01-15 → 2025-10-22 = 0.77 yr, public cadence) ≈ **15 events with no Gaia name**, not the claimed 30-40. Long-tE ≥250 d tail: 2-6 plausible over the 7.6-yr baseline, but each is a multi-year smooth symmetric brightening degenerate with LPV maxima, UXors and slow accretors — and Rodriguez+2022 already published **1,558 "level ongoing" long-timescale candidates**. **Cut from scope: the censored-faint-baseline tail's type claims** (a censored baseline makes fs, u0 and tE degenerate, so PSPL is unfalsifiable exactly where the lane claimed uniqueness) **and the black-hole-lens framing** (telescope-gated in substance).

**Data endpoints.** ZTF DR24 is public (released 2026-01-22, span to 2025-10-22, 5.32e9 light curves) with HATS Lightcurves + Objects at IRSA since 2026-05-26 and on the AWS Registry of Open Data — **bulk parquet/S3 bypasses the per-position IRSA API entirely**, the lane's single best access property. **Entry point must be the DR24 HATS Objects table** (one collapsed-metrics row per object) for the max-minus-median pre-cut, then light curves only for the ~1e4-1e5 survivors; a blind full-light-curve plane scan is multi-TB and not sane on 6 cores. Fink public REST API (no account) for the microlensing module; ALeRCE public API; OGLE-IV EWS static event lists; VSX cone (account live).

**1 day pilot.** Take Zhai+2024's published 124 high-confidence events (machine-readable table) and for each: **(a)** VSX cone search at 3″; **(b)** Fink and ALeRCE object queries; **(c)** TNS cone.

**GATE ZERO.** *If (a) is high, the VSX filing niche is already being worked and the credit channel is gone. If (b) is high — say >60% recovered by the Fink module — then the in-window events are already sitting on a public machine-made list and the lane is **propagation labor wearing a discovery hat → NO_GO by the user's own shelving rule**.* Proceed only if VSX recall ≈ 0 **AND** broker recall is low, or the broker class is demonstrably swamped: measure the class's raw population in the same plane fields — if Fink's microlensing class returns thousands of objects for 2023-2025 against ~60 expected real events, the tag is a bucket, not a list, and separating them is real work.

**Credit channel.** VSX type **"Microlens"** — documented in the VSX type designation manual as an accepted extrinsic type, precedent GSC 03656-01328 = Gaia16aye; the user's VSX account is live and no registration is needed. TNS optional (task #152).

**Biggest risk.** *The yield is real but may be unfilable at scale.* The deliverable is a **type claim**, not an object detection, and the referee is a VSX moderator: Zhai still had to call **54 of 178** merely "possible" with a full pipeline and detection-efficiency simulations. Achromatic-symmetric-single-episode on sparse 2-day g/r photometry will hit dwarf-nova/WZ Sge contamination hard in the plane, throughput at 2-4 h/event plus moderation round-trips is months rather than weeks, and a batch of rejected or later-overturned MICROLENS entries would cost standing in the one credit channel the user has. Clock: DR25 lands **2027-01-20** and hands the incumbents both the next window and the plane partnership data — assume a Caltech sequel within 6-15 months, which removes the publication claim but not VSX filings made first.

## 5. Closing meta-lesson — where novelty actually lives, late 2026

**This scan REFUTES the July-2026 conclusion, but not in the project's favour.** The July claim had two halves: (i) discovery is exhausted, (ii) what remains is propagation labor — the measured-but-never-filed gap between professional catalogues and MPC/VSX/TNS. Both halves have since failed, and it is worth being precise about how.

Half (ii) failed *empirically and expensively*. Propagation labor turned out to be a race the solo loses: **AT 2026uxw was filed by the Fink/ELEPHANT team 24 h 57 m after our package was complete**, at day 20 of a 20-day window; four refereed Rubin consumer packages remain unfiled and therefore, by the project's own standard, untested; the SSOLS backbone was shelved. Propagation labor has a codified credit channel and no moat — the same properties, which is exactly why it loses. Meanwhile the four things that *did* land since July all came from somewhere else.

Half (i) failed *positively*, and the pattern is unambiguous. The filed VSX discovery (ZTF18abxnwmb) exists because scatter-based variability metrics are structurally blind to a shallow, ~5.7%-duty-cycle eclipse — nobody had to fail to file it; the selection function could not emit it. The z~1 turn-on AGN is "structurally invisible to every broker" for the same class of reason: the state change predates the difference-imaging reference, so no alert can exist. The uncatalogued CV with a 4.8-mag state change survived because the alert stream's own reference geometry hid it. **All three are new-to-knowledge objects recovered from censoring — from what the instrument's pipeline cannot express, not from what somebody forgot to submit.** That is discovery, and it happened three times in ten weeks on archives everyone agreed were exhausted.

So the corrected statement is: **the remaining solo surface is CENSORING — the gap between what an archive measured and what its selection function is capable of emitting.** This scan converges on that independently: nine of ten curated lanes are absence-as-signal lanes, generated by different agents, without being told to be.

Three honest qualifications, because this reading flatters the project if left unqualified.

**First, the measured rate is one object per pilot, not per lane.** Not one of the headline yields (100-300, 30-80, 2,000-5,000) has ever materialised; every actual find arrived as pilot bycatch. Budget accordingly: run gate-zero tests, expect 0-3, never build a census on a projection.

**Second, censoring lanes carry a specific pathology that killed or wounded half this batch.** An absence is satisfied by every pipeline defect — masks, blends, edge jitter, catflags, saturation, association-radius edges. The turn-on lane's 9,745-object, zero-candidate null was this, and Lanes 1, 3B, 5-Stage-3 and 9 each rebuilt it in new clothes. The discipline that separates the three successes from those failures is **same-instrument comparison** (ZTF-vs-ZTF, DECam-vs-DECam, Gaia-vs-Gaia) plus a **measured**, not bounded, false-positive floor. Where the comparison crosses instruments, the lane is presumed dead until proven otherwise.

**Third, the professionals are moving into the censoring blind spots right now, and faster than the July picture assumed.** van Roestel+2026 executed the negative-alert channel end-to-end three weeks ago. ASAS-SN is walking up one magnitude slice per year through exactly the deep-fade population. Tzanidakis & Davenport hold 63M FGK stars and the Gaia17bpp archetype. The window on any given corner is **months**, and the only durable edge is the corner an incumbent's *parent catalogue* excludes by construction — van Roestel's 273k white dwarfs, Zhai's ≥30-point rule, the PGIR census's ≥2 mag floor, CU7's requirement of periodicity. Pick the exclusion, not the physics.

**Operationally:** the ranking above is not a work plan, it is a queue of one-to-two-day kill tests. Four of them (Lane 5's single file download, Lane 6's VSX-in-short-windows cross-match, Lane 2's DR3 dry run, Lane 7's 124-event broker recall) cost under a week in total and will close or promote all four GO_CANDIDATEs. Run Lane 2's access check first regardless of ranking — it is 20 minutes, and if anonymous Gaia TAP cannot return >2,000 rows without an account, that fact needs to be known now and not on 2 December.
