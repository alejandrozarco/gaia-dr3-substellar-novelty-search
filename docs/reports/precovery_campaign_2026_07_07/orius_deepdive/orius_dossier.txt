ORBIT/DISCOVERY: a=21.36 AU, e=0.4183, i=17.88, q=12.42, Q=30.30, P=98.7 yr, T_J=3.746.
Perihelion 2007-01-07; currently OUTBOUND, aphelion ~2056. H=9.73 (JPL); 9.725+/-0.343 (Cernis+2016).
JPL orbit #5 (soln 2021), arc 2002-02-12 to 2012-05-19, 84 obs, RMS 0.5", condition code 3.
Discovered 2009-04-25 by K. Cernis & I. Eglitis, Baldone 0.8/1.2m Schmidt (V~20.7). Numbered 2012-06,
named 2013-01 (centaur Orius, killed by Heracles stealing Pholus' wine).

PHYSICAL: only measured quantity is H~9.7-9.9 (V). Size = H+assumed-albedo only: D~26 km(p=0.30) to 79 km(p=0.05),
nominal ~40-63 km; no thermal constraint. Colour class UNKNOWN. Rotation UNKNOWN. Dynamics: diffusing Centaur,
half-life ~4.5-5 Myr, high JFC-transition probability.

OCCULTATION: Centaurs this size ARE occultation targets (only route to real size/shape/rings). Orius is carried
by Lucky Star (Sicardy/LESIA; uses Hv=9.9) and RECON (Buie/SwRI, attempted 2018-07-06 event, star G=14.6).
But orbit is NOT occultation-grade: 2018 RECON prediction had 1sigma time err 576 s, cross-track 2141 km (~1/3 Earth
radius) even 6 yr post last-obs. NOW (2026-04) JPL 3sigma sky ellipse ~14" semi-major = ~230,000 km at Dz=22.4 AU
(~76,000 km 1sigma) = many Earth diameters, effectively unpredictable. Root cause: 84-obs cc-3 2002-2012 arc
extrapolated 14 yr with no post-2012 astrometry in the solution. Occultation needs sub-Earth-radius / mas-level
path -> our 2013-2015 DECam astrometry is the highest-leverage available fix short of a new recovery.

OBSERVABILITY: fading toward aphelion. 2013-15 (our data) r=14.2-15.4 V=21.6-22.0; 2026 r=22.0 V=23.5
(RA~21h15m Dec -34); 2030 V=23.8; 2036 V=24.2; ~2050-56 aphelion V~24.8. Our window was near brightest
post-perihelion apparition; only fainter for ~30 yr. RUBIN/LSST: Dec -34 squarely in footprint (Cerro Pachon,
to Dec ~+15); V~23.5 above single-visit depth (r~24.5) now, stackable into mid-2030s. LSST will give dense
Gaia-referenced astrometry (finally occultation-grade orbit) + sparse multi-band photometry (first real colour,
maybe rotation). Realistically the next dataset to advance this object.

BOTTOM LINE: dynamically well-studied, physically near-unstudied Centaur; orbit too coarse for the occultation
campaigns that list it. Our 6 points matter mainly as a +2.9 yr outbound astrometric arc extension for
Lucky Star/RECON ephemerides, secondarily as an H~9.7-10.1 brightness check (no variability claim possible).
