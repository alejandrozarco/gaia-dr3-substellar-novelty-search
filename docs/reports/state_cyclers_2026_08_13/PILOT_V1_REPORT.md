## Claim verdict

NOT FALSIFIED — but NOT TESTED either: the pilot was instrument-limited, not source-limited. The measurement failed for a specific, now-diagnosed reason: amp_proxy was computed from alert DIFFERENCE-image magnitudes, which are unbounded-high for bright references — so the amplitude ranking flooded the top of the list with flat G=12-16 stars (9/10 verified survivors were exactly this: isdiffpos=-1-dominated subtraction residuals on constant stars; the 10th was TNS-classified SN 2022okv). Meanwhile the archetype class (r~23 quiescence, no bright counterpart, genuine positive-diff amplitudes) compresses to amp_proxy ~2.2 and fell BELOW the de-facto screening intake cutoff of 2.72: run through this pipeline, ZTF19abxfaon itself would have been harvested but never screened. The ranking metric anti-selected the target. Verdict components: (a) the claim's upper half (~150-300 sky-wide) is disfavored at >=95% ONLY under a completeness assumption known to be false — do not bank it; (b) the low end (50) is fully consistent with zero detections even under full completeness; (c) the decisive, cheap test is a corrected-photometry re-rank of the 1,976 cached candidates — the claim stands open until that runs. Do NOT scale the lane to more strips before the re-rank; that would replicate the systematic, not the measurement.

## Caveats

(1) DOMINANT SYSTEMATIC — alert-amplitude bias: amp_proxy from difference-image magpsf is invalid whenever a bright reference sits within ~1 arcsec; 9/10 verified survivors were flat stars (DR ranges 0.02-0.3 mag) with negative-diff-dominated alert streams; the ranking was effectively inverse-sorted for the target class. (2) The archetype's own compressed amp_proxy (2.24) is below the screening intake cutoff (2.72): 1,496 of 1,976 candidates (amp 2.0-2.72, including the 394 in the 2.0-2.24 archetype slice) were never screened against catalogs at all. (3) Verification cap: only 10/40 survivors verified; the 30 unverified are classified artifact-like by FINGERPRINT (Gaia G 12.2-15.6, parallax S/N 10-300, W1-W2 ~ 0 — identical to the verified artifact class; survivor-pool median G=13.1, 34/40 G<16), not by lightcurve forensics — a rule-10-adjacent inference, explicitly flagged as such. (4) 274 catalog kills unaudited; the 30-arcsec VSX/Milliquas kill radius risks false-match over-killing in sparse high-|b| fields (hurts completeness, not purity). (5) Oid duplication: ZTF splits one source across multiple oids; the 1,976 overcounts unique sources by ~10-20%; dedupe was only done within the screened slice. (6) Cadence/censoring: harvest required ndet>=30, span>=700 d, lastmjd>=58894 — recent turn-ons (<2 yr) and heavily alert-censored objects (low detfrac) are excluded or amplitude-compressed by construction; no correction applied. (7) Single-strip cosmic variance unquantified; the factor-2 candidate-density gradient with |b| (9.0 -> 4.25 per deg2 across substrips) says any sky-wide scaling needs |b| weighting, not uniform density. (8) Extrapolation assumes 15,000 deg2 usable footprint (dec > -28, |b| > 20) — stated assumption, not measured. (9) Bookkeeping: 3 Gaia source_id transcription discrepancies in candidate metadata (correct source recovered in each verification); 1 TNS cone unresolved (HTTP 429) on a low-relevance object; ndethist<ndet oddity on 3 harvest_4 objects makes their censor ratios unreliable. (10) All 40 survivor lightcurves and screen/verify intermediates are on disk under /tmp/state_cyclers/ (screen/, verify_*/, harvest_*/) for the re-rank; nothing needs re-harvesting.
